# Mental Health and Wellbeing Locals of Victoria 2026

A single-page reference to Victoria's 20 Mental Health and Wellbeing Locals: locations, lead providers and partners, contact numbers, a searchable map, a comparison table and the rollout timeline.

All information comes from public sources (Victorian Department of Health, Better Health Channel, Premier's media releases, provider announcements), listed at the bottom of the page. Data current as of October 2026.

Not a crisis service directory. In an emergency call 000; for 24-hour crisis support call Lifeline on 13 11 14.

The Victoria outline is from [rowanhogan/australian-states](https://github.com/rowanhogan/australian-states).
